# Play Store listing — Addiyon Keyboard

**Single source of truth for the Play Console listing.** Paste from here; nothing in this
file ships in the APK. Supersedes the old root-level `play-store-listing.md` (deleted).

Primary keywords: *Amharic keyboard, Amharic typing, Ethiopian keyboard, Ge'ez / Geez,
Fidel, transliteration, Ethiopia* — and in Ethiopic: *አማርኛ, ኪቦርድ, ፊደል, ግዕዝ*.

> **Accuracy note.** The privacy wording below is deliberately scoped and must stay
> identical in meaning to `ui/i18n/AppStrings.kt` (`aboutPrivacy`) and to
> `site/privacy.html`. An earlier draft claimed the app "never collects any data" full
> stop, which does not survive contact with the microphone permission. Listing copy that
> contradicts the Data Safety form is a common IME rejection reason.

## Screenshots

Play requires **at least 2** phone screenshots; **4–6 is the practical sweet spot**.
Capture at 1080×1920 or higher, portrait, on a clean device (no notifications, full
battery, plausible clock).

Captions carry real ASO weight — they are what a browsing user actually reads. Burn the
caption into the image; Play provides no caption field for phone screenshots.

| # | Screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mid-transliteration: `selam` typed, ሰላም showing, suggestion strip populated | **Type "selam" — get ሰላም** |
| 2 | Suggestion strip with several Amharic completions offered | **250,000+ Amharic words suggested as you type** |
| 3 | Theme picker with a few palettes visible | **Themes for light and dark** |
| 4 | English QWERTY layout, globe key highlighted | **Amharic and English, one tap apart** |
| 5 | Voice input active, listening state visible | **Speak it — voice typing built in** |
| 6 | Built-in typing manual showing a Fidel family | **Built-in guide for every Fidel** |

Shot 1 is the hook and must be first — it is the only one many users will look at, and it
carries the entire product proposition in a single image.

## ASO notes

- **Where Play indexes:** title > short description > full description. The title carries
  by far the most weight per character — all 30 should be working.
- **Repeat the top keywords 2–4 times naturally** across the full description. The current
  draft does this for "Amharic keyboard", "Amharic typing", "Ge'ez", and "Fidel".
- **Bilingual search matters here.** Diaspora users search in Latin ("amharic keyboard");
  in-country users increasingly search in Ethiopic ("የአማርኛ ኪቦርድ"). The English listing
  cannot rank for the latter, which is why the am-ET listing above is not optional.
- **Do not** keyword-stuff, name competitors (Gboard, SwiftKey, and similar), or use
  superlatives ("best", "#1", "fastest"). All are grounds for listing rejection; the
  competitor names can also draw a policy strike.
- **Ratings move ranking more than copy does.** The in-app review prompt (`review/`, gated
  by a usage counter) is the highest-leverage ASO asset already built — confirm it
  actually fires before launch.
- **Iterate after launch, not before.** Play Console → Store performance reports which
  search terms actually convert. Change one field at a time so the signal stays readable.
