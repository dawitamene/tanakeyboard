Privacy Policy

TextRevamp AI Keyboard for Android · Last updated 13 August 2026

Who this applies to

This policy covers TextRevamp AI Keyboard (com.textrevamp.keyboard). It does not change the separate Addiyon Keyboard policy.

On-device typing

Ordinary key input and word suggestions run on your device. TextRevamp does not transmit every keystroke.

AI rephrasing

When you explicitly request an AI rewrite, the text you selected or chose to rewrite is sent to TextRevamp's server and a paid AI processing provider to generate the result.

Optional phrase completions

Phrase completions are off by default. If you affirmatively enable them, TextRevamp may send up to the last 512 characters before the cursor after you pause typing. The server and its paid AI provider use that text to generate one short continuation.

TextRevamp suppresses phrase-completion requests in password and private fields, email-address and structured fields, selected text, and editors that use Android's personalized-learning or writing-tools opt-outs. You can disable phrase completions from the AI account screen at any time.

Storage and logging

Text sent for AI processing and generated completions are not stored as typing history and are not included in product analytics. Operational records may include request time, model, latency, token counts, status, quota identity, and error category, but not the typed prefix or generated completion.

Accounts and quotas

TextRevamp processes account information used to sign in and an account or anonymous identifier used to enforce daily usage limits. Phrase completions require a signed-in account.

Service providers

AI text is processed through paid model-provider services configured by TextRevamp. Production services are configured so submitted API content is not used to train provider models. Provider security and abuse-monitoring retention may still apply under the applicable paid-service terms.

Your choices

You can use the keyboard and on-device word suggestions without phrase completions, decline the disclosure, turn the feature off, sign out, clear the app's local data, or uninstall the app.

Contact

Questions or account-data requests: keyboard@addiyon.com.
