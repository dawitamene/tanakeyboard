Privacy Policy

Addiyon Keyboard for Android · Last updated 30 July 2026

In short: Addiyon Keyboard never collects, stores, or transmits what you type. Optional usage analytics and crash diagnostics are independent choices and both are off by default. Voice typing hands speech to the recognition service selected on your device. These limited network features are explained below.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the Addiyon Keyboard Android application (com.addiyon.keyboard) published by Addiyon. It does not cover other apps you use the keyboard inside of, or the operating system itself.

What we do not collect

Regardless of your diagnostics choices, we do not collect, log, store, sell, or transmit:

  • the text you type, in any app, at any time;
  • your contacts, precise location, photos, files, clipboard, or email content;
  • suggestions, voice transcripts, target-app identity, field labels, or typing cadence;
  • advertising identifiers, advertising profiles, or custom account/user IDs;
  • any account information — the app has no sign-up, sign-in, or user account.

There is no Addiyon account or typing-data server.

Why typing is private by construction

Typing paths are separated from the app's narrow telemetry interface. That interface accepts only fixed categories and booleans: it has no way to accept characters, words, editor text, suggestions, transcripts, app identity, arbitrary parameters, or exception messages. Private and password fields suppress every custom analytics event.

Transliteration (turning "selam" into ሰላም) and word suggestions run entirely on your device against dictionary files bundled inside the app. Those dictionaries are fixed at install time. They are not personalised to you, and nothing you type is added to them or sent anywhere.

Optional usage analytics and crash diagnostics

The app offers two independent controls under Privacy & diagnostics. Both are off on a fresh install and stay off until you enable them.

  • Usage analytics sends fixed app-lifecycle and feature-category events, such as first use, a keyboard session, chosen keyboard language, opening a layout, or accepting a completion versus a prediction. It can include a Firebase app installation identifier, general device/app information, and approximate region derived by Google from network information. It never includes typed or editor content.
  • Crash diagnostics sends fatal crash information and privacy-sanitized caught-error reports, with app/device state and Firebase or Crashlytics installation identifiers. Caught-error reports use a fixed category, remove the original message, cause, suppressed exceptions, and outside stack frames, and are rate-limited. Out-of-memory errors are not sent as caught reports.

Google Firebase processes this data for Addiyon as our diagnostics service provider. We do not set a Firebase/Analytics user ID, collect an advertising ID, enable Google Signals or ad personalization, link Google Ads, sell this data, or use it for advertising. Data is encrypted in transit.

After Addiyon confirms the choice was saved, turning Analytics off stops future collection and resets its local app-instance data where supported. Turning Crashlytics off stops future collection and deletes locally queued unsent reports. If the device cannot save a choice, Addiyon keeps collection off while the app remains open and asks you to retry before closing it. Disabling either cannot retroactively remove reports already uploaded. Before production launch, the release owner must configure the Firebase Analytics property for two-month user/event-level retention and verify that setting in Firebase Console. That owner-side setup remains a release requirement until it is recorded as complete; the app build cannot configure it. Standard aggregated reports are not controlled by that setting. Firebase retains Crashlytics stack traces and associated installation identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Contact us below with a privacy request or concern.

Permissions the app requests

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — used only for the optional voice typing feature, and only while you are actively dictating. Android asks for your explicit consent before this is ever granted, and the keyboard works fully without it. See the next section.
  • Vibrate (VIBRATE) — used for haptic feedback on keypress, which you can turn off in the app's settings. It involves no data of any kind.
  • Internet (INTERNET) — used only for Firebase diagnostics you opt into. This normal permission does not give the app access to typed content.

The release manifest explicitly removes Advertising ID and Android AdServices permissions.

Voice typing (important)

Voice typing is optional, off by default, and only runs while you hold or activate the microphone key.

When you use it, Addiyon Keyboard does not process your audio itself. It passes the request to the speech-recognition service that is installed and selected on your device — on most phones this is Google's speech recognition, though the manufacturer may provide a different one.

That service may send your audio off your device for processing. Whether it does, what it retains, and for how long is determined by that provider, not by Addiyon. Once we hand the request over, the audio is governed by the privacy policy of the speech-recognition provider on your device — for Google, its Privacy Policy.

Addiyon Keyboard never receives, stores, or transmits a recording. It receives only the recognized text, which is inserted into whatever field you are typing in. If you prefer that no audio leave your device, simply do not use the microphone key, or deny the microphone permission entirely — every other feature keeps working.

What is stored on your device

The app saves your preferences locally, in Android's standard private app storage. This includes: your keyboard language, theme, keyboard height, number-row setting, sound and vibration settings, recently used emoji and skin-tone choices, and a simple counter used to decide when to ask you for a Play Store review.

These preferences stay in the app's private storage and are deleted when you uninstall the app. Diagnostics consent is stored separately from ordinary settings.

Android Backup

If you have Android's built-in backup enabled, Android may include the two preference files above in the backup it makes to your own Google account, so your settings are restored when you set up a new phone. This backup is performed by Android, stored in your Google account, and governed by Google's privacy policy. It contains your settings and recent emoji only. It does not contain typed text, because we never store any. You can disable this in your device's system backup settings.

The diagnostics-consent file is explicitly excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so a restored phone cannot silently inherit consent from another device.

Links to other apps

The app can open a few external destinations at your request: the Google Play listing (when you choose to rate or share the app) and Telegram or your email app (when you choose to send feedback). The app also uses Google Play's in-app review feature, provided by the Play Store app on your device. Choosing any of these hands you off to that app, under its own privacy policy. None of them are contacted unless you tap them.

Children

Addiyon Keyboard is a general-purpose typing tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect typed content from children or anyone else. Optional diagnostics remain off until the device user enables them.
